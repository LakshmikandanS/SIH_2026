Q3 FINANCIAL SUMMARY — INTERNAL DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTED
Prepared by: Corporate Finance
Period: Q3, quarter ended 2026-06-30

REVENUE

Plant 2 operations recorded 14.2 million in segment revenue for the
quarter, against 13.1 million in the prior comparable period. Volume
growth accounted for the majority of the variance; realized pricing
was broadly flat.

OPERATING COST

Maintenance and turnaround spend totalled 2.4 million, above the
2.0 million plan. The overrun is attributed to unscheduled repair
activity across the rotating equipment fleet and to accelerated
procurement of long-lead spare components.

CAPITAL

Approved capital drawdown for the period was 5.8 million, of which
4.1 million was deployed. The remaining balance carries forward.

HEADCOUNT AND COMPENSATION

Segment headcount closed the quarter at 412. Compensation accrual
including the provisional incentive pool stands at 3.3 million.
